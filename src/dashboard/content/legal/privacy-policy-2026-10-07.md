**Effective date: October 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Vouch Commerce, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Vouch," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses and discloses information in connection with the Vouch platform, including the Post to Pay purchase option and the merchant dashboard (together, the "Platform").

This Policy covers the Platform. Our marketing website at vouch.so is covered by a separate privacy policy.

###### 1. Who This Policy Covers

This Policy applies to:

**Influencers.** Individuals who use Post to Pay to place an order with a participating merchant, and individuals who begin a Post to Pay order without completing it.

**Merchant personnel.** Individuals who access the merchant dashboard on behalf of a business.

**A note on roles.** Where we process information from a merchant's store on that merchant's behalf, such as its order and customer records, we act as that merchant's service provider. The merchant decides how that information is used, its own privacy policy applies, and our agreement with that merchant governs our handling of it. Everything else described in this Policy, including all information we collect directly from you when you use Post to Pay, we handle as a business in our own right.

###### 2. Information You Provide

When you use Post to Pay we collect:

- The social media platform you select and the account username you enter;
- Your name, email address and telephone number;
- Your shipping address;
- Your choices at checkout, including whether you grant content usage rights;
- Correspondence you send us, including support requests and appeals.

We do not collect or store your payment card details. Payment is processed by the merchant through its own commerce and payment providers.

###### 3. Information We Collect From Other Sources

**3.1 Social media profile information.** When you enter a username, we retrieve publicly available information about that account from our data providers and from the social media platform. This includes follower count, engagement metrics, posting history and averages, audience characteristics such as estimated location, language, age range and gender distribution, indicators of account authenticity, and other publicly available profile information. We use this to calculate your discount and to assess authenticity and brand safety.

**3.2 Published content.** After your order is delivered, we monitor the account you provided for a qualifying post and record it, and we collect information about that post, including its content, its URL and identifier, and its performance metrics such as views, likes, comments and shares. We continue to collect performance information for a period after publication.

**3.3 Merchant systems.** We receive order and fulfillment information from the merchant's commerce platform, including order identifiers, items, order status, delivery status, and the status of any deferred payment.

###### 4. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Platform we collect device and usage information, including IP address, browser type, operating system, referring pages, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your activity. We also record the version of our terms you accepted, the time of acceptance, and the information displayed to you at the time.

###### 5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Operate Post to Pay, including evaluating the account you submit, calculating your discount, and recording and reviewing your post, including any flag the merchant raises about it;
- Fulfill your order in conjunction with the merchant, including communicating order and delivery status;
- Send you reminders about your posting window and deadline by email and, where you have provided a mobile number, by text message;
- Provide the merchant dashboard, including reporting on orders, posts and performance;
- Provide support and respond to your correspondence, including appeals;
- Detect, investigate and prevent fraud, misuse and breaches of our terms, and enforce restrictions on access;
- Maintain records of terms acceptance and transactions;
- Comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
- Maintain the security and integrity of the Platform;
- Analyse use of the Platform and improve, develop and support our existing and future products and services, including analytical and scoring products.

###### 6. How We Disclose Information

**6.1 Merchants.** We disclose information about you to the merchant with whom you place an order, including your name, contact details, shipping address, order information, the social media account you provided, profile and audience information relating to that account, your published post and its performance, the outcome of any review of your post, and the content rights you granted. Merchants are contractually restricted in what they may do with profile and audience information we source from our data providers, and may not sell it or make it available to third parties.

We do not disclose the results of an account evaluation to a merchant, together with your handle, before you complete checkout.

**6.2 Service providers.** We disclose information to service providers who process it on our behalf, including our social media data providers, commerce and payment platforms, communications providers, hosting and infrastructure providers, analytics and error monitoring providers, and support tooling. A list of our current service providers is available on request.

**6.3 Professional advisors and investors.** We may disclose information to our accountants, lawyers, insurers and other advisors, and aggregated or de-identified information to current and prospective investors.

**6.4 Legal and safety.** We may disclose information where we believe it is required by law, or reasonably necessary to enforce our terms (including by reporting a post to the social media platform on which it was published), to protect the rights, property or safety of any person, or to investigate fraud or misuse.

**6.5 Business transfers.** We may disclose information in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganisation or sale of assets, or in an insolvency proceeding.

###### 7. Sale and Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under California law.

If that changes, we will update this Policy, give notice before the change takes effect, and provide the opt-out mechanisms required by law. We do not sell or share the personal information of anyone we know to be under sixteen years of age.

###### 8. Aggregated and De-Identified Information

We may create aggregated and de-identified information from the information described in this Policy, and may use and disclose it for any purpose, including research, benchmarking, marketing, and developing and offering products and services. We maintain such information in de-identified form and do not attempt to re-identify it, except as permitted by law.

###### 9. Restriction Records

Where we restrict a person's access to Post to Pay, we retain a record of that restriction, and information associated with the relevant orders, for as long as necessary to enforce it. Because these records exist to prevent fraud and misuse across all merchants that accept Post to Pay, we retain them indefinitely, and they may not be deleted on request where retention is permitted by law for fraud prevention and security purposes.

###### 10. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy.

- Account evaluation results and profile information are retained while an order is active and for a period afterwards to support reporting and dispute resolution.
- Post and performance information is retained for as long as we report on it to the merchant and for a period afterwards.
- Order and transaction records, and records of terms acceptance, are retained for the period required by applicable law and for the resolution of disputes. If you ask us to delete your information, we keep the record of an order until 180 days after the order is closed, so that payment disputes and chargebacks can be resolved, and then remove your social media handle, profile information and contact details from it. The amounts, dates, order reference and terms you accepted are kept.
- Restriction records are retained as described in Section 9.
- Information we process on a merchant's behalf is retained as described in our agreement with that merchant.

###### 11. Security

We maintain technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse and alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

###### 12. Your Rights

**12.1 Rights available.** Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you and how we use and disclose it, to obtain a copy of it, to correct inaccurate information, to delete it, to opt out of any sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

**12.2 How to exercise them.** Email support@vouch.so from the address associated with your order, or write to us at the address in Section 16. We will verify your request by reference to information we already hold. An authorised agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authority.

**12.3 Response and appeal.** We will respond within the period required by applicable law. If we decline your request, we will tell you why. Where the law where you live gives you a right to appeal that decision, you may appeal by replying to our response, and we will inform you of the outcome and of your right to contact your state attorney general.

**12.4 Limits.** We may decline a request where an exception applies, including where we are required to retain information by law, where retention is necessary to detect or prevent fraud or security incidents, or where deletion would compromise the integrity of a transaction record. Section 9 describes one such case.

**12.5 Categories.** In the twelve months preceding the effective date of this Policy, we collected the categories of personal information described in Sections 2, 3 and 4, from the sources described in those Sections, for the purposes described in Section 5, and disclosed them for a business purpose to the categories of recipients described in Section 6. We did not sell or share personal information.

**12.6 Requests about merchant customer records.** If your request concerns information we process on a merchant's behalf about you as its customer, please contact that merchant. We will assist the merchant in responding.

###### 13. Communications

**13.1 Text messages.** If you provide a mobile telephone number when placing a Post to Pay order, we may send automated text messages about your order and your posting deadline. Reply STOP to any message to stop them. Message and data rates may apply.

**13.2 Email.** We send email about your order. Any marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe link, which we honour promptly. Transactional email about an order you have placed cannot be unsubscribed from while that order is open.

###### 14. Children

The Platform is not directed to children, and Post to Pay may only be used by individuals aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

###### 15. Third-Party Platforms and Links

Your use of a social media platform is governed by that platform's own terms and privacy policy, not ours. Where the Platform links to a merchant's store or to a third-party site, that site's own privacy practices apply, and we are not responsible for them.

###### 16. Where Information Is Processed

The Platform is operated from the United States and is offered to users located in the United States. We store information in the United States. Some of our service providers, including social media data providers, may process information in other countries.

###### 17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. When we do, we will update the effective date above. If we make a material change, we will provide additional notice where appropriate, and where the change requires it, we will obtain consent or provide an opt-out before it takes effect.
